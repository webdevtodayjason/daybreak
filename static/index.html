One pocket-sized AI appliance. Seven days. The entire world's news cycle.

DAYBREAK is a live stress test disguised as an intelligence terminal. We took a Tiiny Pocket — a palm-sized edge device with an NPU — and instead of benchmarking it with synthetic loops, we gave it a job a real operations team would recognize: read everything, miss nothing, around the clock, for a week straight.

THE JOB

Fourteen free OSINT feeds — world wires, conflict reporting, CISA cyber advisories, seismic alerts, humanitarian updates — pour in 24/7. Every single item is processed on the device. No cloud API is called. Ever.

  • ORNITH-1.0-35B reads each story and writes the one-line analysis: category, combatant-command region, severity S1–S5.
  • QWEN3-EMBEDDING converts every item to vectors; cosine clustering fuses cross-source coverage into DEVELOPING stories, which Ornith then titles.
  • Z-IMAGE-TURBO paints a photorealistic editorial image for any article you click — 8 diffusion steps, ~8 seconds, rendered by the same pocket NPU.

All three models are resident simultaneously — 83 of the device's 100 NPU units — scheduled around each other in real time. You can watch it think in the AI OPS LOG.

THE TEST

This is a kick-the-tires endurance run: does an edge AI appliance hold up under sustained, unattended, production-shaped load for seven days? Thermals, throughput, memory, network, model stability — all of it is on this board, live, warts and all. The hardware panel is the telemetry; the wire is the proof of work.

THE RIG

Tiiny Pocket (CIX NPU, 49GB device memory) does the thinking. An Orange Pi 6 Plus in the server rack runs ingestion, this dashboard, the IR rack camera, and the Cloudflare tunnel. Backend is pure Python stdlib. The front end is one file.
